About Me

I'm Faiz — a passionate Full Stack Web Developer with a deep love for building things that matter. I architect web applications that are fast, scalable, and visually stunning, from interactive front-ends to robust back-end systems.

Beyond the web, I moonlight as a Game Developer, crafting engaging experiences in browser-based environments and exploring what Unreal Engine can push to its limits.
